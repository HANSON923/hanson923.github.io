Toy Adventure

A two-player co-op game set in a playroom. You and a friend are two small toys among giant ones, picking up, throwing and dropping blocks as you explore.

Watch Play Credits

Two players, one playroom

The screen is split down the middle, one half per player. Each player can pick up a block, then throw it or drop it.

My part: theming and UI

On a team of four, I was responsible for the game’s theming and its UI.

That includes the main menu. This page borrows the same look: the wooden frame, the cream board and the menu buttons.
