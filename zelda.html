On top of that grid I wrote how each enemy moves. Pick one below to see it in action.

Custom level: Timestop

Our custom mechanic is the Timestop: a clock gun that freezes enemies for a long stretch. In the original game, bombs are rarely worth using because enemies move too fast to stay in the blast. Freeze them first, then bomb them, and the two items become a combo.

Stopping time slows the usual fast-paced combat down, so players can take their time and think about the room they’re in. That made space for simple puzzles that wouldn’t fit the high-speed action of the original.

The custom level spans several rooms, each built around this mechanic.

2D Bowroom

In the original game, the bow sits in a side-scrolling passage beneath the dungeon. I built that room: the view switches from top-down to a 2D side view, and Link climbs ladders and dodges Keese to reach the bow before heading back up.

Two very different views sharing one dungeon show how much a game’s look shapes the way players read it.
